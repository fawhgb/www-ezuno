Information Collection and Use

You can contact us by email. Depending on your request, the information you choose to send may include your name, email address, company, number of users, licensing information, technical environment and a description of your question or support issue.

The contact, free-license and support buttons open a prepared message in your own email application. You can edit the message before sending it. This website has no online forms and does not submit or store the contents of these drafts. We receive your email address and message only if you send the email.

No customer account or login is required to browse this website. Your browser still sends the technical connection data needed to deliver the requested pages and files to the hosting provider.

Any of the information we collect from you may be used in one of the following ways:

  • To personalize your experience (your information may help us to better respond to your individual needs)
  • To improve our Website or Service and our Support Center (we continually strive to improve our website offerings based on the information and feedback we receive from you)
  • To improve customer service (your information helps us to more effectively respond to your customer service requests and support needs)
  • To process transactions (your information, whether public or private, will not be sold, exchanged, transferred, or given to any other company for any reason whatsoever, without your consent, other than for the express purpose of delivering the purchased product or service requested)
  • To send periodic E-mails (the E-mail address you provide may be used to send you information and updates pertaining to your order, in addition to receiving occasional company news, updates, related product or service information, etc.) Note: If at any time you would like to unsubscribe from receiving future emails, send a request to ezuno@ezuno.com.

Log Data

This static website is hosted using GitHub Pages. To deliver pages and downloads and operate and secure the hosting service, GitHub receives technical request data such as your IP address, the requested URL, date and time, and information sent by your browser. We do not operate our own visitor analytics or a contact database in this website.

This website does not load Google Analytics or other analytics scripts. Fonts, images, stylesheets and the website script are served locally from the same website.

Cookies, local storage and optional video

The website code does not set cookies and does not use local storage or session storage.

The optional video is provided by Vimeo. No Vimeo player is loaded until you choose to play the video. After that click, your browser connects to Vimeo and transmits connection data, including your IP address. The player uses Vimeo's do-not-track setting; Vimeo may still process data needed to provide the video. You can use the rest of the website without playing it.

Service Providers

GitHub provides the website hosting. Email enquiries are handled through our email service and our sales or support processes. If you choose to play the video, Vimeo provides the player. These services may process personal data as part of providing their services.

Information about the hosting provider is available in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Information about the optional video provider is available in the Vimeo Privacy Policy.
